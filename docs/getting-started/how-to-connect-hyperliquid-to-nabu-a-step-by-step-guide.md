# How to connect Hyperliquid to Nabu: a step-by-step guide

## Step 1: Start the connection in Nabu

1\. Go to [app.nabu.pro/?settings=connect](https://app.nabu.pro/?settings=connect). This opens the **Connect** tab of your Nabu settings.

<figure><img src="../../assets/images/settings-connect.jpg" alt="Nabu settings with the Connect tab open, showing the Exchange Connect form with the Connect Exchange Account selector and the Link Exchange button" width="480"></figure>

2\. Under **Connect Exchange Account**, select **Hyperliquid**.

## Step 2: Export your private key from Privy

3\. Go to [home.privy.io/account](https://home.privy.io/account) and, under **Linked Accounts**, add your **Google account** (the same one you use for Nabu).

<figure><img src="../../assets/images/hyperliquid-step-3-privy-account.png" alt="Privy account page with Linked Accounts" width="308"></figure>

4\. Go to [home.privy.io/apps](https://home.privy.io/apps) and select **Nabu** from the apps list.

<figure><img src="../../assets/images/hyperliquid-step-4-privy-apps.png" alt="Privy apps list with Nabu selected" width="412"></figure>

5\. Click **Export keys** and choose the account connected to your Nabu account.

<figure><img src="../../assets/images/hyperliquid-step-5-export-keys.png" alt="Privy Export keys dialog" width="624"></figure>

6\. Click **Copy key**.

<figure><img src="../../assets/images/hyperliquid-step-6-copy-key.png" alt="Privy Copy key action" width="624"></figure>

## Step 3: Import the key into your wallet

7\. Open **MetaMask** (or any other wallet), click on **Accounts**, then **Add a wallet**.

<figure><img src="../../assets/images/hyperliquid-step-7-add-wallet.png" alt="MetaMask Add a wallet screen" width="192"></figure>

8\. Choose **Import an account** and paste your copied private key.

## Step 4: Connect to Hyperliquid and deposit funds

9\. Go to [app.hyperliquid.xyz](https://app.hyperliquid.xyz/) and connect with the wallet containing your imported account.

10\. Click **Deposit** and deposit **USDC on Arbitrum** into your Hyperliquid account.

11\. Check the **Balances** tab on Hyperliquid to confirm your funds have arrived.

<figure><img src="../../assets/images/hyperliquid-step-11-balances.png" alt="Hyperliquid Balances tab showing deposited funds" width="624"></figure>

## Step 5: Link your account in Nabu

12\. In your wallet, open the imported account and click **Account details**.

<figure><img src="../../assets/images/hyperliquid-step-12-account-details.png" alt="Account menu for the imported wallet account, with Account details highlighted" width="261"></figure>

13\. Copy your **private key** and return to the **Connect** tab at [app.nabu.pro/?settings=connect](https://app.nabu.pro/?settings=connect). With **Hyperliquid** selected under **Connect Exchange Account**, paste the key into the **Private Key** field. Check that the **Wallet address** shown below the field matches your imported account, then click **Link Exchange**.

<figure><img src="../../assets/images/hyperliquid-step-13-private-key.jpg" alt="Connect Exchange Account form with Hyperliquid selected, a masked private key, the derived wallet address, and the Link Exchange button" width="450"></figure>

14\. Hyperliquid now appears under **Connected** with a green status indicator and your wallet address.

<figure><img src="../../assets/images/hyperliquid-step-14-connected.jpg" alt="Connected list showing Hyperliquid with a green status indicator, the wallet address, and a remove action" width="450"></figure>
