# Get help with Nabu

Read this page before you open a request. Most answers are already in the docs, and a complete request gets a faster reply.

**Reply time:** up to two business days. Weekends take longer.

## Before you open a request

1. Connecting an exchange? Follow the setup guide for [Hyperliquid](../getting-started/how-to-connect-hyperliquid-to-nabu-a-step-by-step-guide.md) or [Binance](../getting-started/how-to-connect-binance-to-nabu-a-step-by-step-guide.md) step by step first.
2. Search the docs for any term you are unsure about. The [glossary](../glossary.md) explains [PSL](../glossary.md#psl), [DEX](../glossary.md#dex), [CEX](../glossary.md#cex), and the rest.
3. Still stuck? Send a request through the [form](request.md). Email is the fastest way to reach the team.
4. Problems with the exchange account itself, such as KYC or a locked account, go to that exchange's support. Nabu cannot see or change your exchange account.
