# How to connect Hyperliquid to Nabu: a step-by-step guide

## Step 1: Start the connection in Nabu

1\. Go to [app.nabu.pro/settings](https://app.nabu.pro/settings).

2\. In the first tab, under Connect Exchange Account, select Hyperliquid.

<figure><img src="/assets/images/hyperliquid (3).png" alt="" width="624"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Step 2: Export your private key from Privy

3\. Go to [home.privy.io/account](https://home.privy.io/account) and, under Linked Accounts, add your Google account (the same one you use for Nabu).

<figure><img src="/assets/images/hyperliquid (10).png" alt="" width="308"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4\. Go to [home.privy.io/apps](https://home.privy.io/apps) and select Nabu from the apps list.

<figure><img src="/assets/images/hyperliquid (6).png" alt="" width="412"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5\. Click Export keys and choose the account connected to your Nabu account.

<figure><img src="/assets/images/hyperliquid (9).png" alt="" width="624"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6\. Click Copy key.

<figure><img src="/assets/images/hyperliquid (1).png" alt="" width="624"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Step 3: Import the key into your wallet

7\. Open MetaMask (or any other wallet), click on Accounts, then Add a wallet.

<figure><img src="/assets/images/hyperliquid (7).png" alt="" width="192"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8\. Choose Import an account and paste your copied private key.

<figure><img src="/assets/images/hyperliquid (5).png" alt="" width="338"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Step 4: Connect to Hyperliquid and deposit funds

9\. Go to [app.hyperliquid.xyz](https://app.hyperliquid.xyz/) and connect with the wallet containing your imported account.

10\. Click Deposit and deposit USDC on Arbitrum into your Hyperliquid account.

11\. Check the Balances tab on Hyperliquid to confirm your funds have arrived.

<figure><img src="/assets/images/hyperliquid (2).png" alt="" width="624"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Step 5: Link your account in Nabu

12\. In your wallet, open the imported account and click Account details.

<figure><img src="/assets/images/hyperliquid (4).png" alt="" width="261"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

13\. Copy your private key, return to the Nabu settings page, and paste it into the Private Key field and click Link Exchange.

<figure><img src="/assets/images/hyperliquid (8).png" alt="" width="624"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
